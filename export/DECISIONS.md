## Message to the SDR after the first return (Mayank sends; sent 2026-10-05)

Thanks for the first seven. Four small things for the next companies, so the timings and the
scoring work:

1. Stamp each company on its own: Step 1 start when you begin it, Step 1 end when you finish the
   first pass, Step 2 start and Step 2 end for the second pass. Please do not copy the Step 1 times
   into Step 2, and do not use one start and end for several companies.
2. After Step 1 on a company, copy that whole row (as values) into the sheet "Pass 1 snapshot
   (paste only)", then carry on with Step 2 on the RESEARCH sheet.
3. The Y/N product cells take Y or N only. If you cannot tell, leave the cell blank.
4. A Dead end needs one of the four kinds in the dropdown. If you simply cannot find information
   on a company, it stays a Prospect and the cells you could not fill stay blank.

The grey columns (Order, Company ID, Company name, Market, Arm) are set for you; please do not edit
or clear them. The same four points are printed at the end of the Instructions sheet in the next file.
